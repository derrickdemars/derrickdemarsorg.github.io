Explain

Here is why the values in your table matched.

{{ uRight }} − θ

Side a is opposite θ and adjacent to {{ uRight }} − θ.

In a right triangle, the two acute angles add to {{ uRight }}. If one is θ, the other is {{ uRight }} − θ.

The side opposite θ is the same side that is adjacent to {{ uRight }} − θ. Both angles share the hypotenuse.

So opposite / hypotenuse for θ and adjacent / hypotenuse for {{ uRight }} − θ are the same fraction, a / c.

The cofunction relationship
sin θ = cos({{ uRight }} − θ)
cos θ = sin({{ uRight }} − θ)

Where the name comes from

Cosine is short for the Latin complementi sinus, "sine of the complement." The cosine of an angle is the sine of its complementary angle.
