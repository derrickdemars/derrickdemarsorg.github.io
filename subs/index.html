How IXL Works in My Classes

These guidelines apply to every class with IXL assignments. I want students to get the most productive practice they can.

If a student gets badly stuck on one skill, they should move on to another one and come back later. When a student feels stuck, make sure they're using their binder. It should be open to the worksheet that mirrors the IXL problems they're working on, so please encourage them to use it.

If they're in groups, encourage them to ask each other for help. They can also watch the IXL instructional videos if they get stuck. Headphones are in the back of the room if they don't have their own. Students who finish their assignments early should stay in their group and help their peers.

End of Day: Homeroom

At the end of the day, homeroom (the Geometry class) comes back to the room and tidies up a bit. They put up their chromebook and get the classroom ready for the next day. They know what to do. If it's the end of the week, they'll also update the weekly calendar.
