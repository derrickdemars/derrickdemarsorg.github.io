Experience

I teach 7th Grade Math through Algebra III. I built each of my courses myself and matched them to state standards. My students have grown on benchmark tests every year, and I was named Teacher of the Year in 2024–2025 and again in 2025–2026. I hold a PhD in mathematics and have helped train a student teacher.

Teaching approach

Challenging work and support go together. Every lesson has more than one way in, extra help that fades as students grow more confident, and feedback clear enough to act on. Clear structure gives every student a strong start without limiting how far they can go.
