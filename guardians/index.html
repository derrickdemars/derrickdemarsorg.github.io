How grades work

Each term grade is made of three parts.

Minor grades

40%

IXL skills, warm-ups, and in-class assignments.

Major grades

45%

Quizzes, presentations, and projects.

Term exam

15%

One exam at the end of each term.

The binder

How it’s organized

Each binder has two parts. Worksheets go in the front, newest first, so the latest work is the first thing your child sees. Behind a divider, the back holds memory aids, rules, procedures, and other materials that vary by course.

How worksheets work

Worksheets come with more practice problems than we finish in class. In class, Dr. DeMars shows how to solve them, the class solves some together, and then students practice on their own. The extra problems are there on purpose, so your child can keep practicing at home, on their own, with you, or with a tutor.

Where it lives

Binders have a set place in the classroom, and we use them in class nearly every day. Students are welcome to take them home, as long as they bring them back for class. Students are permitted to use their binders on most warm-ups, quizzes, and other assessments, so a complete, organized binder is one of the best tools for success in Dr. DeMars’s class.

How to use it well

  • vertical_align_topPut each new worksheet on top, in front of the last one.
  • bookmarksKeep memory aids and other reference pages behind the divider.
  • editWrite a printed name and the date on every page.
  • quizBefore a quiz or test, work the extra problems and review that topic’s memory aids.
  • homeWhen the binder comes home, look through it together, and make sure it’s back in time for class, since we use it nearly every day.

IXL at home

80

A skill is complete when your child reaches a SmartScore of 80 or higher.

  1. 1Have your child sign in to IXL with their own login, then go to ixl.com/dashboard (opens in a new tab).
  2. 2Open the From your teacher tab. These are the skills Dr. DeMars assigned. The newest are at the top and the oldest are at the bottom.
  3. 3Look for a number in parentheses after a skill, like (45). That’s the SmartScore so far. No number means the skill hasn’t been started.

Retakes, late work, and extended time

Retakes

  • replayIf your child fails a test, they get one retake. The two scores are averaged, and the average goes in the gradebook.
  • calendar_monthYour child sets up a time for the retake with Dr. DeMars.

Absences and late work

  • event_availableWork and tests missed because of an absence, excused or not, are due within the same number of school days your child was absent.
  • scheduleWork turned in after the due date loses one letter grade (10 points, so an 85 becomes a 75). This includes IXL skills.

Extended time

  • more_timeIf your child has an extended-time accommodation, they get one extra class period to finish in-class quizzes and tests.
  • assignmentExtended time doesn’t apply to other assignments. Those are due by Dr. DeMars’s deadlines.
