How grades work

Each term grade is made of three parts.

Minor grades

40%

IXL skills, warm-ups, and in-class assignments.

Major grades

45%

Quizzes, presentations, and projects.

Term exam

15%

One exam at the end of each term.

IXL at home

80

A skill is complete when your child reaches a SmartScore of 80 or higher.

  1. 1Have your child sign in to IXL with their own login, then go to ixl.com/dashboard (opens in a new tab).
  2. 2Open the From your teacher tab. These are the skills Dr. DeMars assigned. The newest are at the top and the oldest are at the bottom.
  3. 3Look for a number in parentheses after a skill, like (45). That’s the SmartScore so far. No number means the skill hasn’t been started.

Late skills: once a late skill reaches 80, it’s recorded as the SmartScore minus 10. For example, a late skill finished at 90 is recorded as 80. Until it reaches 80, it shows as Incomplete in Gradelink.

Retakes, make-ups, and late work

  • replayIf your child fails an assessment, they get one retake. The average of the two scores becomes the recorded grade.
  • event_availableIf your child misses an assessment, they can make it up within the same number of school days they were absent.
  • calendar_monthRetakes and make-ups are scheduled with Dr. DeMars.
  • scheduleLate work follows the same rule as make-ups: it’s due within the same number of school days your child was absent.

Studying for tests at home

All memory aidsarrow_forward
event

Watch the calendar

Quizzes and tests show as filled items on the calendar above. Each course page also shows the next one at the top. Subscribe to get them in your phone’s calendar app, for one class or several.

schedule

Start a few days early

Short practice over three or four nights sticks better than one long session the night before.

lightbulb

Use the memory aids

Each course page lists its memory aids. They open as PDFs on a phone or Chromebook. Ask your child to explain one to you in their own words.

menu_book

Bring the binder home

Binders usually stay in class but can come home for extra practice. Students can use their binders on most warm-ups and quizzes, so a complete, organized binder is a real study tool.

checklist

Finish the IXL skills

The skills assigned during a unit practice the same topics as the quiz or test.

record_voice_over

Quiz out loud

Cover part of a memory aid and ask your child to fill it in. Saying an answer out loud helps it stick.
